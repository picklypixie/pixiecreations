Glow

Your skin is not a category

Oily and resilient is not the same problem as oily, sensitive and barrier-damaged. PixieFits builds a profile from both a questionnaire and a photo, and treats them as one picture rather than two opinions.

What a photo can actually tell you

One selfie gives redness, texture, pores, oil and pigmentation — read on your phone, not on a server. The numbers sit next to what you told us about your own skin, because the mirror and the questionnaire disagree often enough to matter.

Scan a product label and PixieFits reads the ingredients against that profile, so "is this going to break me out" has an answer before you buy it.

Why the three fit together

Separately they are three apps you would forget about. Together they are a loop that gets better the longer you use it.

Your body tells it how clothes behave

How a cut sits on your proportions, and which silhouettes do the work you want.

Your skin tells it how products behave

What your barrier tolerates, what it needs now, and what can wait until later.

Your goals decide the direction

The same profile points at different clothes depending on what you want today.

Questions

Do my photos leave my phone?

The analysis itself runs on the device — the model is in the app. A skin scan is saved to your own account so you can compare it later, and you can delete it. Nothing is posted anywhere unless you choose to share it. The detail is in the privacy policy.

Is there a free version?

Yes. Some features need a subscription, billed through the App Store — you can cancel it there at any time.

Can I delete everything?

Yes — in the app, under account settings. It removes your profile, your scans, your closet and your posts, and closes the account.

What is Diary by PixieFits?

A companion app for recording what you actually wore. It shares this account and this closet — the same records, not a copy.
